​💧 Drink It, Fool! — Cute 3D Glass Hydration Widget
​Drink It, Fool! puts a gorgeous, living 3D water glass right on your Android home screen with just enough attitude to actually make you drink water. Built for pure daily vibes with zero ads, zero clutter, and zero data tracking, it turns every sip into a tiny moment of fun.
​✨ Why You'll Love It
​🦆 Sassy Floating Buddies: Pick your favorite companion—a Rubber Ducky, Axolotl, Chubby Frog, or Boba Pearl—and watch them float toward the rim as you drink up. Leave them stranded at the bottom of a dry cup for too long, and they will judge you.
​🍓 Dynamic Fruit & Ice Drops: Every tap drops a refreshing garnish into your 3D glass, from clinking ice cubes to strawberries, lemon slices, cherries, and mint leaves.
​😤 Dramatic Glass Moods: Keep your glass happy and sparkling ( ≧ ◡ ≦ ) ✨ with regular sips, or watch it pull a dramatic thirsty face ( ；´Д｀) 💦 and fire off a cute reminder if you ignore it for 2.5 hours.
​🌅 Living Time-of-Day Glow: The frosted 3D glass shifts its ambient glow throughout the day—from bright morning sunshine to warm golden-hour hues and a cozy midnight nightlight.
​🫧 Tactile Splash & Clink Haptics: Feel and hear every sip with a custom double-pulse ice-clink vibration, a bubble-pop sound effect, and a celebratory fanfare when you hit 8/8 sips.
​🕛 12:00 AM Fresh-Slate Wipe: No databases, no guilt trips over yesterday's count, and no permanent storage. At midnight sharp, the glass pours itself out with a sparkle reset so you start fresh every single morning.
